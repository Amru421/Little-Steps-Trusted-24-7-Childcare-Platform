THE APP TOUR

What you’ll see

  1. Find care: Search by city, age group, and 24-hour availability.
  2. Compare centers: Review care hours, caregiver introductions, safety details, and listed plans. Verify details independently before booking.
  3. Request a place: Check availability and send an hourly, daily, or monthly request. A provider responds in the app.
  4. Manage a center: Providers update hours, capacity, pricing, caregivers, and booking decisions.
  5. Review responsibly: Administrators review accounts and provider-submitted center details.

Center and certification details in the demo may be sample information. This version does not collect payments, upload verification documents, or provide live CCTV.
